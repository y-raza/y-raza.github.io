Who We Are

Senior Leadership.
Every Engagement.

TriFusion Solutions is a boutique IT management consulting firm in Fairfax, Virginia. We work with state and local organizations, nonprofits, and mid-market companies that need experienced technology leadership without hiring a full-time executive.

Most consulting firms sell you a senior name and staff the work with junior people. At TriFusion, the principal you hire is the person who does the work. You get one accountable leader, a defined scope, and plain answers.

Our work comes from twenty years of leading IT modernization and delivery programs. We tell you what we see, what it will cost, and what to do first.

What We Do

Three Ways to Work With Us

Each offer has a fixed scope and a price agreed before work starts.

01
IT & AI Readiness Assessment
2 to 4 weeks

For organizations that need to know where their technology stands and what to do about AI.

  • Review of systems, vendors, security posture, and data
  • Screen of AI use cases and the governance gaps that come with them
  • A prioritized 12-month roadmap with cost ranges
  • A leadership briefing for your executive team or board
02
Vendor Selection & Contract Review
Fixed fee, scoped per purchase

For organizations about to buy a system or renew a large contract.

  • Requirements, shortlist, and a scoring model you can defend
  • Review of statements of work, pricing, and service levels
  • Negotiation support through signature
  • No reseller agreements and no vendor fees, so the advice stays yours
03
Fractional IT Governance & PMO Advisor
Monthly retainer, set hours

For organizations without a CIO or program office that still run real IT spend.

  • Oversight of projects, vendors, budgets, and risks
  • Status reporting your leadership can read in five minutes
  • Delivery governance from kickoff to closeout
  • A senior voice in the room when decisions get made
